Three sketches, each a guestbook page and a memory book. Same fictional guests as every other sketch. Every sketch takes ?n=3 / ?n=300, ?theme=dark, and ?bar=0 (hide the dev bar); the bar's params drawer is built with the site's own buildControl(). Sign the guestbook (or add ?fire=1) to see the burst.

1. guestbook.cgi

A 1999 personal homepage, done with love: tiled pixel-heart wallpaper, WordArt, a marquee, an under-construction strip ("this marriage is under construction — completion date: never"), a hit counter, a webring, 88×31 badges and canon_in_d.mid, synthesised. Each entry is the guestbook.cgi table. The book is the page printed from Navigator: "Page 2 of 7", the URL and the date in the margins.

2. cut-and-paste

A photocopied riot-grrrl zine. Ransom-note headings, every entry a clipping cut from a different paper (bond, notebook, newsprint, graph, a black photocopy, construction paper) keyed off its id, signed in marker, date rubber-stamped. Signing runs the page through the copier. The book is a real zine: paginated by measurement and imposed for saddle-stitch — print double-sided, fold, staple.

3. tractor-feed

By day a dot-matrix printout: each entry a torn-off chunk of green-bar continuous form, sprocket holes punched, text struck as pins. By night the same records on a phosphor BBS terminal. Signing prints a receipt. The book opens with a Print Shop banner of the couple's names running sideways across three pages — tape them up — then the whole guestbook as a green-bar listing, strips and bars on every sheet.
