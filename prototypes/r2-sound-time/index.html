Design round 2 · guestbook & memory book

Sound, time, space

Three sketches that use the one thing only the guestbook has, the guests’ own words, and give it a dimension the rest of the site doesn’t have. Each sketch has a guestbook view and a printable memory book. All of them use the shared fictional fixture.

  1. Player Piano

    Every name is spelled in notes. The guestbook is one long punched roll, and your scroll is the crank: holes crossing the brass bar light up, and play if you turn sound on. The memory book is the score: every guest engraved as a line of sheet music, with the letters of their name as the lyrics.

    card roll · background tracker · burst punch · book score

  2. The Long Day

    The whole guestbook laid on one 24-hour day by the minute each note was written. The page opens at your own hour, under that hour’s sky, and each card is lit by the real sun over Portland when it was written. The ink ages with the anniversaries. The memory book is a Book of Hours.

    card sundial · background daylight · book hours · try the ⏱ Time machine, or the first anniversary at 10 pm

  3. The Stair

    Notes hung on threads around a spiral stair in CSS 3D. Scroll to walk down it; the far notes show their signer’s name on the back. It is flat and still under reduced motion. The memory book is the spiral: every name on one continuous line, from the first to sign out to the last.

    card hung · background stair · book spiral
