How to play this book

Every name in this book is spelled in notes. The letters run from A to G and then start again, so H is A, I is B, and O is A once more. Play the notes left to right with one finger, on any piano, starting near middle C. The lyrics under each staff are the letters of the name, so you can sing along.

The rhythm comes from the message: a short word is a quick note, a long word is a long one. A chord at the end means the note was loved (it had hearts); a small diamond above is a bell, for a favourite.
